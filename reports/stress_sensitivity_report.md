This report evaluates the portfolio credit loss sensitivity when borrower leverage and property equity feature clusters are stressed simultaneously.

## Key Takeaways
1. **Maximum Stress Exposure**: Under severe double-trigger stress conditions (Severe Leverage + Severe Equity), the average portfolio default probability reaches **38.81%**.
2. **Equity Buffer**: High LTV increases default risk even under Base Borrower Leverage scenarios, moving default risk from **38.83%** to **38.83%**.
